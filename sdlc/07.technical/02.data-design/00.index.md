và flow"*, không phải tên bảng.

**Rules that hold here:** `project` is the **leading key part** on every table · `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE` on evidence and on
`source_claim` · `jsonb` only for parts that are never filtered · `visibility` is **computed**, never a column ([`D-45`](../../03.inception/02.decision-log/00.index.md)).
