## Two layers — do not confuse them

| Layer | Where | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **rules** — what a gate is, what a mark means, what a document looks like | [`../03.sdlc-standard/`](../03.sdlc-standard/00.index.md) | normative, **never filled in** |
| **forms** — apply the rules to **one** slice / **one** increment | **this folder** | filled in and ticked |

**Do not copy rules into forms.** If a rule is missing, fix it in the standard.
