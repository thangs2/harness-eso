| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **numbers must be counted, never estimated** | three separate totals were wrong in one session (82 vs 83, 22 vs 23, "391 tokens") |
| **every row must have at least one "owes"** | a row with none is finished — and nothing is finished |

| **debt must have an owner** | ownerless debt is never paid |

## 6. Falsification

1. **Numbers here disagree with a fresh count.** ⇒ the file is decoration; it must be generated, not written.
2. **A row has no debt.** ⇒ either finished (none are), or **someone is hiding debt**.
3. **An AC is added without a scenario.** ⇒ the `SCN` column is where that shows up.
4. **Debt with no owner.** ⇒ never paid.
