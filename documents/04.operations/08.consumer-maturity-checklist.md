## 4. What baplatform has beyond “rules / graphs / md / jira”

Operators often list only four items. Full gold surface also includes:

1. **Host pack + Observatory tenancy** (ports, sidecar, decision ledger)  
2. **Cognitive preflight** HOT/WARM/COLD + HMAC write gate  
3. **Dual-Zone SDLC** (`00.agent-manifests` vs `01..12`)  
4. **Context contract** domain → INV pinning  
5. **Token-Diet + conversation hygiene** turn budget  
6. **Engineering SLP 7 workers** + verify/audit gates  
7. **Brain stamp** + **`brain-dispatch` skill** before protected writes  
8. **Jira Execution Pack** + Zero-SDLC-Crawl (T5) + lean `delivery.workflow` stub  
9. **QC lane** (`workflow.qc-runtime`) after qualified DEV + **`workflow.agentic-factory` HIR**  
10. **eso-coverage / host-install integrity** scorecards
